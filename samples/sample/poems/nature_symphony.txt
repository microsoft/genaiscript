A symphony hums in the cool night air,  
As moonlight dances in the forest’s lair.  
The whispering leaves, the rustling trees,  
Compose a melody sung by the breeze.  

The river murmurs its endless song,  
Through valleys deep and cliffs so strong.  
The crickets chirp in rhythmic delight,  
A choir unseen in the velvet light.  

Mountains stand tall, cloaked in misty grace,  
Guardians of time in their ancient embrace.  
Stars above, like lanterns hung high,  
Illuminate dreams in the boundless sky.  

Oh nature, your rhythms soften the soul,  
A symphony eternal, making hearts whole.  
With every note, you gently remind,  
We’re part of this song, forever entwined.
